## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: specific at the front door, generic behind it. The doors, the key screen and "Save your key" could not be lifted into another product: three doors, "declared, not checked", "works like a password", vouching not ID. After onboarding it is stock community SaaS: a threaded-comments widget with one flag emoji as the whole transnational signal, an interchangeable profile card, four identical pathway cards. North star 2 (collaboration across borders, felt) has nowhere to live yet.

Deterministic scan: source scan over 31 files returned 0 findings, but regex-only (element rules need a rendered page). Rendered scans (22 captures, 11 states × 2 themes) cluster in three families: (1) brand-blue contrast, 3.68:1 white-on-blue and 3.52:1 blue-on-gray-50, on every primary and secondary control — locked, report only; (2) `ai-color-palette` on the doors/keys page wrapper (blue→violet gradient background, BetaDoorsPage.module.scss / LoginPage.module.scss), both themes; (3) three stragglers (doors lead at 390px, profile-step lead "later.", hub "instead." ×2, forum subtitle "test."). Dark-only, not locked: placeholders #757575 on #1e293b at 3.17:1 (keys, profile step, edit modal); "Add photo" 3.97:1; "Select your country" #6b7280 on #0f172a 3.69:1. Light-only: upcoming stepper numerals 4.41:1. Borderline badges 4.34:1 light / 4.47:1 dark. Mechanical: no horizontal overflow anywhere, exactly one h1 on every state, targets under 44px: Notifications bell 34×34, Edit profile 122×32, stepper circles 32×32 (parent 80×32), backup checkbox 20×20 inside a 44px label.

Where they agree: blue secondary labels ("Copy key", "Open the forum", "+ Add sources"). What the detector missed: the fabricated participation rows, the off-screen Continue, the icon-only generate, Enter-posts. False positives: the off-canvas skip link, disabled controls (WCAG exempts them), the 1×1 hidden file input, text-occlusion rows from a double injection.

## Overall Impression

The skeleton is right and the copy is honest. The problems are of placement and of leakage: the demo leaks into the beta (fabricated participation, deliberation vocabulary, Server URL, AI key, sources), and the two moments that decide whether an unaided tester finishes (making a key, saving it) hide their primary action. The single biggest opportunity: give the unaided run a finish line that is one named ask, not a wall of four equal pathways under "0 of 4 · Unverified".

## What's Working

1. Honest copy at the high-stakes moments: "Right now the key works like a password: whoever types it in is you." / "Declared, not checked, in this test." / "no ID papers, no face scans".
2. The onboarding skeleton: 4-step stepper with ticks, heading focus per step, aria-live announcements, resumable progress, "You're all set up" on revisit. Dark mode complete on every captured surface.
3. Keyboard and focus are done: visible 2px rings on every tab stop tested, Post correctly skipped while disabled, the menu traps focus and closes on Escape.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Profile card shows fabricated activity. DigitalAgentCard.tsx renders DEMO_PARTICIPATION unconditionally: "Clean drinking water for all · Problem · 2d ago" and two more on a brand-new tester's own profile, with stage badges from the hidden pipeline. Why: a lie about the user on the beta's only trust-building surface. Fix: in beta render "No activity yet" or drop the block (gate on isBeta). Command: /impeccable harden.
2. [P1] "Save your key" hides Continue and under-signals success. At 360×740 Continue sits at y≈740 because .flowMain keeps $footer-clearance for a StageFooter the beta never renders; the checkbox is the last visible thing, so ticking it appears to do nothing. "Copied to the clipboard." is $success-dark on $gray-50 ≈3.7:1 in light. Fix: drop the clearance in beta, keep Continue in the first viewport, make the copied state a success Banner under the button pressed. Files: OnboardingFlow.module.scss, steps.module.scss .keyStatus, BackupStep.tsx. Command: /impeccable layout.
3. [P1] Keys screen: the newcomer's one required action is an unlabeled grey refresh icon, and the error goes stale. After "Join as a person" the screen shows a disabled Get Started, a Server URL field, and the only way forward is the icon-only generate button; tapping it after a blur leaves the red error while the CTA turns blue. Fix: when a door is recorded, primary CTA "Create my key" (generate + sign in), demote "Paste a key you saved" to a disclosure, hide Server URL behind the (i) in beta, clear publicKeyError in generateRandomKey, echo the door in the title. File: LoginPage.tsx. Command: /impeccable onboard.
4. [P1] Verification hub offers four equal pathways with no recommended one, two of them camera and data heavy. The brief's flow is "four people who know you vouch", but the recommendation lives only in the empty state below the cards. Fix: in beta, "Ask a member" as the single primary card; the other three under "Other ways"; one camera/data line once. Files: PathwayCards.tsx, VerificationHub.tsx. Command: /impeccable distill.
5. [P2] Forum composer behaves like a deliberation tool. Textarea has no accessible name (placeholder only), Enter submits (Android users post fragments), "+ Add sources" is jargon, count reads "3 comments" under Post, Delete has no confirm, sort toggle shows for one post. Fix (variant="forum" in ThreadedDiscussion.tsx): aria-label the textarea, Enter = newline and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter = post, hide SourcesInput, beta.forum.* wording for count/status, confirm Delete, hide sort below three posts. Command: /impeccable clarify, then /impeccable harden.

Not locked, mechanical, cheap: dark placeholders 3.17:1 (keys, profile step, edit modal); dark "Select your country" trigger 3.69:1; dark "Add photo" 3.97:1; light upcoming stepper numerals 4.41:1; Notifications bell 34×34 and Edit profile 32px tall; the blue→violet gradient wrapper on doors/keys (detector: ai-color-palette) — a brand-blue-only ground would clear it.

2. Door on screen 1 and Kind on step 2 are the same decision asked twice; with a door recorded, Kind could be a one-line confirm with "change".

4. Desktop: column-width white title block under a full-bleed white bar reads as a floating rectangle.
5. Ready step has ≈350px of empty space between the recap and the CTAs; "Next: ask 4 people who know you" would use it.
6. "Your language" next to an English-only app invites "why is it still English?"; label it "Language you speak".
7. Generate button in dark is near-invisible ($dark-surface on a $dark-bg card, secondary-text glyph).

## Locked, report only

White-on-$primary 3.68:1 on every primary CTA and $primary text on white/gray-50 for secondary buttons and links (Eston's brand decision). System-sans typography throughout. The 4-stage IA and demo HomeView are untouched by the beta. English-only beta by decision.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the beta is "the network, not the pipeline", why does the unaided run end on "0 of 4 · Unverified" with four doors? Should Ready end on one named ask ("Send your first vouch request to someone who knows you") so there is a finish line?
2. The forum is the only place a Kenyan and an Indian tester meet, and the whole transnational signal is a 16px flag. What would "12 people from 5 countries are here" in the forum header take: a seam read, or a UI change on data the forum already has?
3. The audience often shares phones. Is a key on paper (word list or QR) the honest beta backup, and should "I have saved my key" be a test the product runs (paste it back) rather than a checkbox?
