## Open design questions to resolve in the brainstorm (with my leanings)
- **★ How the vote card sources the expert-reviewed solutions + their commitments/metrics (the load-bearing carry).** The ballot runs on a **separate** `qv_contract.py` whose `Proposal` is only `{id,text,author,timestamp}` — it does **not** type commitments/metrics. The initiative's `approval.ts` proposals contract is the canonical home of the spine. **Lean: the vote card reads the spine from the `approval` (proposals) contract** (`approvalApi.getProposals` on `stageKey:'proposalsContractId'`, the initiative as parent) — selecting the **expert-reviewed** solutions (those with `expertReviews.length > 0`) — while the `qv` contract continues to own the actual hearts/allocation. Join the two by proposal text/order. *Alternative:* seed commitments/metrics onto the `qv` proposals too (the seeder already `{...p}`-spreads `propProposals.slice(0,4)` into `qvProposals`, so the fields ride along at runtime even though `qv.ts` doesn't type them) and read them straight off `qv.getProposals`. **Decide which is the clean carry** — this is why S5 precedes S6. Confirm.
- **How many expert-reviewed solutions to show / seed.** Roadmap says "the 5 expert-reviewed solutions," but S4 seeds **2** expert reviews per proposals-initiative and `qv` seeds **4** proposals. **Lean: show "the expert-reviewed solutions" (count = however many have `expertReviews`), and bump the S4-style seed so the vote-stage demo initiatives (privacy is at `stage:'vote'`) carry a realistic set (e.g. 3–4 reviewed, longer/nuanced bodies).** Confirm the exact demo seed (and note `privacy` has no commitments/reviews seeded yet — S4 only seeded `amr`/`jobs`).
- **The 6-region colour scheme + key.** Define the regions (e.g. Africa / Asia-Pacific / Europe / Latin America & Caribbean / North America / Middle East & North Africa) and map ISO countries → region → one `$stage-*`-adjacent token each. **Lean: a new `src/utils/regions.ts` (`regionOf(countryCode)` + `REGIONS` with token-pure colours), reused by the results bars + a bottom key; keep `getCountryColor` for anything else.** Confirm the region set + that the colours stay token-pure and AA in light/dark.
- **Results gating = lock once cast.** **Lean: after a member submits their allocation, the ballot becomes read-only (results visible, controls disabled, an explainer line) — no re-allocation.** Confirm whether it's hard-locked (can't change ever) or just defaults to the results view with a visible "you've voted" state.
- **Completion threshold rule.** **Lean: "Votes from X% of community" where X ≈ 75%, counted as distinct allocators / community member count, read-only (no gate), reusing the S4 threshold bar.** Confirm the exact %.
- **Hearts icon.** **Lean: `QVFlow` already imports `Heart` — confirm the hearts read as filled/iconified hearts at the redesign's visual bar; minor polish, not a rebuild.**
- **Remove the Results tab vs keep it?** S4 removed the Proposals/Results `SegmentedControl`. The vote card has a `proposals`/`allocate`/`results` `SegmentedControl`. **Lean: keep a ballot/results toggle (voting genuinely has two modes) but fold "proposals" away — the ballot IS the solution list now.** Confirm.
- **Layout at 360px.** The redesigned ballot (scope/SDG forehead + the expert-reviewed solutions with commitments + metrics + hearts/usage + regions results + the completion threshold + lock-once-cast). Resolve in the brainstorm (visual companion for the card + the regions key).
